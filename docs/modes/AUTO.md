# AUTO

> **Status:** IN DEVELOPMENT

AUTO is the supervised autonomous-behavior layer of DIABLO X3-NX.

## Current direction

- Autonomous movement requires safety permission.
- LiDAR and vision information are used by higher-level decisions.
- Room-depth and obstacle information can influence movement choices.
- Behavior logic continues to evolve through real-robot testing.

AUTO is **not presented as a finished autonomous navigation stack**.

## Media

**Planned.** Real-robot AUTO demonstrations will follow when the behavior is ready to show publicly.
